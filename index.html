FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Growth Trainer.

What is Growth Trainer?
Growth Trainer is a mobile app designed specifically for personal trainers. It lets you set custom training and nutrition plans for your clients, manage them, and track their fitness progress — all from your phone.
Who is Growth Trainer for?
Growth Trainer is built for personal trainers. If you train clients 1:1 or in small groups, you can assign programs, manage nutrition, and track progress from one app. It is not a consumer workout tracker.
How many exercises does Growth Trainer include?
Growth Trainer includes a library of over 4,000 exercises, each with GIF animations and detailed video demonstrations focused on proper biomechanics.
Can I set nutrition and training plans for my clients?
Yes. Growth Trainer lets you assign personalized training and nutrition plans to each client, so you can manage their workouts and diet guidance from a single app.
How much does Growth Trainer cost?
Growth Trainer is free to download on iOS and Android. Growth Premium costs $59 per month and includes unlimited clients, exclusive programs, advanced analytics, personalized trainer tools, and an ad-free experience.
What platforms is Growth Trainer available on?
Growth Trainer is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Download links are available at the top and bottom of this page.
How does Growth Trainer keep clients motivated?
Growth Trainer groups your clients into a club with its own ranking. Monthly and yearly leaderboards track records and crown a winner each month, so training becomes friendly competition. That community is what sets Growth Trainer apart from other personal training apps.
