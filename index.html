FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Growth Trainer.

What is Growth Trainer?
Growth Trainer is a mobile app designed specifically for personal trainers. It lets you set custom training and nutrition plans for your clients, manage them, and track their fitness progress — all from your phone.
Who is Growth Trainer for?
Growth Trainer is built for personal trainers, fitness coaches, and gym professionals who want to deliver personalized training programs and monitor client progress efficiently.
How many exercises does Growth Trainer include?
Growth Trainer includes a library of over 500 exercises, each with GIF animations and detailed video demonstrations focused on proper biomechanics.
Can I set nutrition and training plans for my clients?
Yes. Growth Trainer lets you assign personalized training and nutrition plans to each client, so you can manage their workouts and diet guidance from a single app.
How much does Growth Trainer cost?
Growth Trainer is free to download on iOS and Android. Growth Premium costs $59 per month and includes unlimited clients, exclusive programs, advanced analytics, personalized coaching tools, and an ad-free experience.
What platforms is Growth Trainer available on?
Growth Trainer is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Download links are available at the top and bottom of this page.
